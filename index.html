🔧 How Lazy_AMPR Works

You don't need to understand how compression works inside. But if you're curious, here's the simple explanation:

1

Finding patterns

LZ4 looks for repetitive patterns in your game files. Games often have lots of repeating data, which can be stored more cleverly.

2

Smart shrinking

Instead of storing the same piece of data many times, it stores it once and remembers where to put it back. This cleverly reduces the file size.

3

Keeps everything intact

When you need to play a game, it can be restored to its original state quickly. Or you can keep the compressed version if you have a decompression tool. Your choice!

📝 Important note: Compression works best on games with lots of repetitive content. Some games may compress only a little, while others shrink dramatically. It's always worth trying!
